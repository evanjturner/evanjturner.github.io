### Project Overview
I am currently proposing my M.S. Thesis on developing an ultrasound-based low-fidelity computational fluid dynamics simulation pipeline to assess whether wall shear stress metrics may be able to screen postpartum mothers for risk of developing hypertensive disorders of pregnancy.

I presented preliminary results on this workflow with a small sample size at the ASME Summer Bioengineering Conference in 2025 and have attached my poster below. The technique shared in the poster provides a general outline of the methodology, but I have since refined the scope of this work to address data collection inconsistencies I found following this conference presentation.
