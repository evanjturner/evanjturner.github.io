### Electric Motorcycle
I worked on a team design project in undergrad to create a concept electric ‘motorcycle’ that would be inexpensive and wouldn't require a motorcycle license to ride (making it an e-bike). I helped source the required electrical components and performed all the modeling work based on the selections our team made. I took aesthetic inspiration from the Maeving RM1, a classy urban commuter bike designed in the UK. Every part of the assembly was modeled in SolidWorks from scratch, however, no FEA was performed to validate the design choices (this was not for an engineering course). Such an analysis would be necessary for further development.
